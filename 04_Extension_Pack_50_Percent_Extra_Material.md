# 50% Extra Extension Materials (Buffer Bank)
**Target Profile:** Overseas Division & International Sales  
**Total Value:** ~30 Minutes of Additional High-Impact Material  
**Usage:** Deploy if the student finishes the diagnostic quickly, needs deeper grammar reinforcement in commercial contexts, or demonstrates high conversational fluency.

---

## 🗣️ Extra Module 1: Situational International Sales Dilemmas (15 min)

### Scenario A: The Overseas Distributor Discount Crisis (Bargaining & Modals)
> **Situation:**  
> Your primary distributor in an overseas market contacts you urgently. Because of recent currency fluctuations and rising shipping costs in their region, they claim they cannot remain profitable unless you grant an immediate **15% price reduction** on all vehicle/equipment orders for the rest of the year. If you refuse, they hint they will start sourcing from a competitor in Asia.
>
> **Questions for Discussion:**
> 1. What options do you have? What **must** you verify before responding?
> 2. What **could** be the risks of giving in immediately vs. standing firm on the contract price?
> 3. If you were leading this negotiation, what counter-offer **would** you propose to keep the client without hurting company margins?

*Teacher target:* Modals of deduction & possibility (*must, could, might, should*) and 2nd conditional bargaining (*"If we offered a rebate based on annual volume, would you be willing to...?"*).

---

### Scenario B: Entering a High-Potential Emerging Overseas Market (Strategy & Predictions)
> **Discussion Prompts:**
> 1. When your company enters a new overseas territory, is it better to work with an **exclusive local distributor** or establish a **direct sales presence**? Why?
> 2. How do you think international supply chains and cross-border trade **will change** over the next 5 to 10 years?
> 3. What cultural differences have you noticed when communicating with clients in Europe vs. Asia or the Americas?

*Teacher target:* Future predictions (*will definitely, is likely to, might, won't*) and comparative language (*far more risky, significantly easier*).

## 🛠️ Extra Module 2: Commercial Conditionals Rapid-Fire Drill (10 min)

*Have the student complete each commercial stem spontaneously with realistic business logic.*

1. **If the overseas client signs the purchase order before the end of the month,**  
   $\rightarrow$ *we will...* ___________________________________________________________

2. **If freight rates continue to rise over the next quarter,**  
   $\rightarrow$ *our export margins will...* ________________________________________________

3. **If our management agreed to a 60-day payment term,**  
   $\rightarrow$ *foreign buyers would...* _________________________________________________

4. **If you had the chance to visit any overseas market next month,**  
   $\rightarrow$ *which country would you...* ________________________________________________

5. **If a shipment arrived damaged at a foreign port,**  
   $\rightarrow$ *what would be your first step?* ___________________________________________

---

## ⚙️ Extra Module 3: Active $\rightarrow$ Passive Commercial Transformations (10 min)
*In contracts, formal proposals, and logistics updates, passive voice creates objective, diplomatic distance.*

1. *"Our export logistics team dispatches every shipment within 48 hours of payment."*  
   $\rightarrow$ **Passive:** Every shipment __________________________________________________

2. *"Customs officers held the container at the border due to missing documentation."*  
   $\rightarrow$ **Passive:** The container __________________________________________________

3. *"The finance department is currently reviewing the foreign partner's credit limit."*  
   $\rightarrow$ **Passive:** The foreign partner's credit limit ________________________________

4. *"We have already credited the disputed amount to your overseas account."*  
   $\rightarrow$ **Passive:** The disputed amount _____________________________________________

#### Answer Key for Module 3:
1. *is dispatched within 48 hours of payment (by our export logistics team).*
2. *was held at the border due to missing documentation.*
3. *is currently being reviewed (by the finance department).*
4. *has already been credited to your overseas account.*

## 📌 Optional Homework / Takeaway Assignment (5 min agreement)
*Give this to the student at the end of class to bridge directly into Lesson 2.*

**Commercial Email Prompt:**  
Write a brief professional email (6–8 sentences) to an overseas client who requested a price reduction on a pending order.  
*Requirements:*
- State your position politely (*use at least one indirect phrase, e.g. "We would like to clarify..."*).
- Propose a conditional compromise (*use a 1st or 2nd conditional, e.g. "If you increase the order volume to X, we can consider..."*).
- Use at least **one Present Perfect** sentence regarding past cooperation (*e.g. "We have valued our partnership since..."*).
