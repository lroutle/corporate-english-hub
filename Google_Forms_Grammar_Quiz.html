1. Right now our sales department _______ a key contract with an overseas distributor, but normally we _______ regional retail accounts. *
Correct: B (is negotiating / handle)
2. While the export team _______ the logistics documents, the foreign buyer suddenly _______ to amend the payment terms. *
Correct: C (were preparing / asked)
3. I _______ with overseas distributors for five years now, but before joining this department I _______ domestic clients. *
Correct: B (have worked / managed)
4. "_______ to a major international trade fair in Germany or the Middle East?" — "Yes, I _______ our company stand at an expo in Frankfurt two months ago." *
Correct: A (Have you ever been / attended)
5. Our export manager looks exhausted because she _______ with our partners in Singapore nonstop since 6:00 AM. *
Correct: C (has been negotiating)
6. "Our client just sent an email refusing our shipping terms." — "Don't worry, I _______ their representative right away to clarify the situation." *
Correct: A (will call)
7. Under international export law, our department _______ provide an official certificate of origin before the shipment can clear customs. *
Correct: B (must / has to)

Correct: B (orders / will provide)

Correct: B (had adapted / wouldn't have lost)

Correct: C (whose)

Correct: B (renewing / to accept)
14. "Could you clarify _______?" *
Correct: B (when the overseas shipment will arrive)
15. Our team is responsible _______ developing overseas partnerships, and our quarterly targets depend heavily _______ market stability. *
Correct: C (for / on)
