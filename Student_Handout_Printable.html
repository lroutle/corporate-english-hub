📌 The Present Perfect vs Past Simple Rule for Client Updates

Past Simple (Specific / Finished Time) Present Perfect (Open Time / Current Status)
• yesterday, last month, in 2024, three days ago
• "We sent the revised quote yesterday."
• "The shipment departed on Tuesday." 		• so far, already, yet, since Monday, this week
• "We have already sent the revised quote."
• "Has the client confirmed the PO yet?"

🤝 Conditional Concession Phrases for International Negotiations

1st Conditional (Concrete Offer):
"If you increase your order volume to 500 units, we will grant a 5% rebate."

2nd Conditional (Hypothetical Exploration / Safe Bargaining):
"If we were to offer 60-day credit terms, would you be willing to sign an exclusive deal?"

Diplomatic Linking Words:
"Provided that / As long as payment is secured via Letter of Credit, we can waive the handling fee."
