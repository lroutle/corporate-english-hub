# Diagnostic Grammar Check (B1 - B2 Level)
**Student Worksheet — International Business & Sales Context**

### Part 1: Multiple Choice & Sentence Completion (15 Questions)
*Choose the correct option (A, B, C, or D) or write the correct form of the verb in brackets.*

#### 1. Routine vs Current Project
Right now our sales department _______ a key contract with an overseas distributor, but normally we _______ regional retail accounts.  
A) negotiates / are handling  
B) is negotiating / handle  
C) negotiates / handle  
D) is negotiating / are handling  

#### 2. Narrative Past & Interrupted Action
While the export team _______ the logistics documents, the foreign buyer suddenly _______ to amend the payment terms.  
A) prepared / asked  
B) were preparing / was asking  
C) were preparing / asked  
D) have prepared / asked  

#### 3. Finished vs Unfinished Time
I _______ with overseas distributors for five years now, but before joining this department I _______ domestic clients.  
A) worked / have managed  
B) have worked / managed  
C) am working / managed  
D) have been working / have managed  

#### 4. Commercial Experience vs Specific Past Deal
"_______ to a major international trade fair in Germany or the Middle East?"  
"Yes, I _______ our company stand at an expo in Frankfurt two months ago."  
A) Have you ever been / attended  
B) Did you ever go / have attended  
C) Have you ever gone / attended  
D) Did you ever go / attended  

#### 5. Duration of Ongoing Negotiations
Our export manager looks exhausted because she _______ with our partners in Singapore nonstop since 6:00 AM.  
A) is negotiating  
B) negotiates  
C) has been negotiating  
D) had negotiated  

#### 6. Future Intentions & Instant Commercial Decisions
"Our client just sent an email refusing our shipping terms."  
"Don't worry, I _______ their representative right away to clarify the situation."  
A) will call  
B) am calling  
C) am going to call  
D) call  

#### 7. Modals: Trade Rules & Regulations
Under international export law, our department _______ provide an official certificate of origin before the shipment can clear customs.  
A) should  
B) must / has to  
C) can  
D) might  

#### 8. First Conditional (Commercial Deal)
If the overseas distributor _______ the full container load today, we _______ a 7% volume discount.  
A) will order / provide  
B) orders / will provide  
C) orders / provide  
D) will order / will provide  

#### 9. Second Conditional (Hypothetical Bargaining)
If our company _______ more flexible credit terms to foreign buyers, we _______ much faster market share abroad.  
A) offers / will gain  
B) offered / would gain  
C) offered / will gain  
D) would offer / gained  

#### 10. Third Conditional (Past Missed Opportunity - Challenge)
If we _______ our sales pitch to their local business culture, we _______ the distribution tender last year.  
A) adapted / wouldn't lose  
B) had adapted / wouldn't have lost  
C) would adapt / didn't lose  
D) had adapted / wouldn't lose  

#### 11. Passive Voice in Commercial Contracts
All export shipments _______ strictly according to Incoterms before they _______ to the port of entry.  
A) insure / dispatch  
B) are insured / are dispatched  
C) are insuring / are dispatched  
D) were insured / dispatched  

#### 12. Relative Clauses
This is the foreign distributor _______ warehouse network covers the entire Latin American market.  
A) who  
B) which  
C) whose  
D) whom  

#### 13. Gerund vs Infinitive in Negotiations
The client considered _______ the contract for another two years, but they refused _______ the revised price index.  
A) to renew / accepting  
B) renewing / to accept  
C) to renew / to accept  
D) renewing / accepting  

#### 14. Indirect / Diplomatic Questions
"Could you clarify _______?"  
A) when will the overseas shipment arrive  
B) when the overseas shipment will arrive  
C) when arrives the overseas shipment  
D) when does the overseas shipment arrive  

#### 15. Dependent Prepositions in Sales
Our team is responsible _______ developing overseas partnerships, and our quarterly targets depend heavily _______ market stability.  
A) of / on  
B) for / in  
C) for / on  
D) to / at
