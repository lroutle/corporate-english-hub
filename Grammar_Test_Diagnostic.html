Diagnostic Grammar Check (B1–B2)

Specialization: International Business Development, Logistics & Overseas Sales

PRIMA LINGUA Corporate Training
Evaluation Benchmark: CEFR B1/B2
Part 1: Multiple Choice & Syntax (15 Questions) 15 Points

Select the best option (A, B, C, or D) to complete each commercial sentence accurately.

1. Right now our sales department _______ a key contract with an overseas distributor, but normally we _______ regional retail accounts.
Grammar focus: Routine vs. Current Ongoing Deal
Answer: B. Temporary action happening "right now" takes Present Continuous; permanent routine ("normally") takes Present Simple.
2. While the export team _______ the logistics documents, the foreign buyer suddenly _______ to amend the payment terms.
Grammar focus: Past Continuous (Background) vs Past Simple (Interruption)
Answer: C. The continuous ongoing process ("were preparing") was interrupted by a single completed action ("asked").
3. I _______ with overseas distributors for five years now, but before joining this department I _______ domestic clients.
Grammar focus: Unfinished Period (Present Perfect) vs Finished Period (Past Simple)
Answer: B. "For five years now" indicates a duration connecting past to present (Present Perfect). "Before joining" is a completed past era (Past Simple).
4. "_______ to a major international trade fair in Germany or the Middle East?" — "Yes, I _______ our company stand at an expo in Frankfurt two months ago."
Grammar focus: General Life Experience vs Specific Past Time ("two months ago")
Answer: A. General life inquiry uses "Have you ever been"; specific finished past time marker ("two months ago") requires Past Simple ("attended").
5. Our export manager looks exhausted because she _______ with our partners in Singapore nonstop since 6:00 AM.
Grammar focus: Present Perfect Continuous (Duration & Visible Result)
Answer: C. Highlights continuous duration ("since 6:00 AM") with a visible present effect ("looks exhausted").
6. "Our client just sent an email refusing our shipping terms." — "Don't worry, I _______ their representative right away to clarify the situation."
Grammar focus: Spontaneous Sales Decision (Will vs Going to)
Answer: A. Instant spontaneous decision made at the moment of speaking takes "will".
7. Under international export law, our department _______ provide an official certificate of origin before the shipment can clear customs.
Grammar focus: Strict Legal/Trade Requirement vs Polite Advice
Answer: B. Legal obligations and mandatory trade rules require "must" or "has to", not soft recommendation ("should").
8. If the overseas distributor _______ the full container load today, we _______ a 7% volume discount.
Grammar focus: First Conditional (Commercial Offer)
Answer: B. First conditional: If + Present Simple ("orders"), will + base verb ("will provide"). "Will" is never used in the if-clause.
9. If our company _______ more flexible credit terms to foreign buyers, we _______ much faster market share abroad.
Grammar focus: Second Conditional (Hypothetical Commercial Bargaining)
Answer: B. Hypothetical scenario in the present/future: If + Past Simple ("offered"), would + base verb ("would gain").
10. If we _______ our sales pitch to their local business culture, we _______ the distribution tender last year.
Grammar focus: Third Conditional (Past Missed Opportunity / Tender Review)
Answer: B. Unreal past situation: If + Past Perfect ("had adapted"), would have + past participle ("wouldn't have lost").
11. All export shipments _______ strictly according to Incoterms before they _______ to the port of entry.
Grammar focus: Passive Voice in Shipping Contracts & Procedures
Answer: B. Shipments receive the action: Present Passive ("are insured", "are dispatched").
12. This is the foreign distributor _______ warehouse network covers the entire Latin American market.
Grammar focus: Possessive Relative Pronouns
Answer: C. Indicates possession (the distributor's warehouse network = "whose warehouse network").
13. The client considered _______ the contract for another two years, but they refused _______ the revised price index.
Grammar focus: Gerund vs Infinitive Complementation
Answer: B. "Consider" takes a gerund (-ing: "renewing"); "refuse" takes an infinitive ("to accept").
14. "Could you clarify _______?"
Grammar focus: Diplomatic Indirect Questions (Word Order)
Answer: B. Embedded/indirect questions follow affirmative word order (Subject + Verb: "the shipment will arrive"), without subject-auxiliary inversion.
15. Our team is responsible _______ developing overseas partnerships, and our quarterly targets depend heavily _______ market stability.
Grammar focus: Dependent Prepositions in Corporate Roles
Answer: C. Standard fixed prepositions: "responsible for" and "depend on".
Part 2: Executive Error-Spotting Challenge (5 Questions) 5 Points

Each sentence contains ONE common corporate English mistake. Write your correction in the box.

16. "I am working in the overseas division since January 2021."
Target Correction: "I have worked (or have been working) in the overseas division since January 2021." (Present Perfect for action from the past continuing until now).
17. "We need to discuss about the pricing policy before the meeting with our foreign client."
Target Correction: "We need to discuss the pricing policy..." (Remove 'about' — discuss is transitive).
18. "If the distributor will sign the contract by Friday, we will dispatch the first batch."
Target Correction: "If the distributor signs the contract by Friday..." (No 'will' in conditional if-clause).
19. "I look forward to meet you at the international trade exhibition next month."
Target Correction: "I look forward to meeting you..." ('to' is a preposition here, followed by gerund -ing).
20. "The sales director explained me the new commission structure."
Target Correction: "The sales director explained to me the new commission structure." (Explain takes preposition 'to' before the person).
