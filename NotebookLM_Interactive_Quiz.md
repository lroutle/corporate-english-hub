# Grammar Quiz

## Question 1
Our regional sales team normally _____ direct inquiries from South America, but this month they _____ a new CRM platform for client management.

- [x] handles / are implementing
- [ ] is handling / implement
- [ ] handles / implement
- [ ] is handling / are implementing

**Hint:** Differentiate between a permanent business routine and a temporary ongoing project.

## Question 2
We _____ five regional distributors in Asia so far this year, whereas in 2023 we _____ only two.

- [x] have signed / signed
- [ ] signed / have signed
- [ ] have signed / have signed
- [ ] signed / signed

**Hint:** Compare an unfinished time period leading up to the present with a completed past year.

## Question 3
While our compliance team _____ the customs clearance forms, the shipping line suddenly _____ a delay at the port.

- [x] was reviewing / announced
- [ ] reviewed / was announcing
- [ ] was reviewing / was announcing
- [ ] reviewed / announced

**Hint:** Identify which event serves as the ongoing background action and which acts as a sudden interrupting event.

## Question 4
The export manager looks exhausted because he _____ with the foreign buyers since early morning.

- [x] has been negotiating
- [ ] is negotiating
- [ ] negotiated
- [ ] negotiates

**Hint:** Focus on the continuous action that started in the past and directly impacts the present moment.

## Question 5
If the foreign client _____ the licensing agreement before 5 PM, our legal department _____ the final documentation tomorrow.

- [x] approves / will release
- [ ] will approve / will release
- [ ] approves / releases
- [ ] will approve / releases

**Hint:** Recall the rule regarding verb tenses in conditional time clauses referring to future possibilities.

## Question 6
If our supplier _____ lower minimum order quantities, we _____ larger overall order volumes per quarter.

- [x] offered / would commit to
- [ ] offers / would commit to
- [ ] offered / will commit to
- [ ] had offered / committed to

**Hint:** Think about the conditional structure used for hypothetical or imaginary commercial negotiations.

## Question 7
If we _____ our shipping schedule to their preferred lead times last quarter, we _____ the contract.

- [x] had adjusted / wouldn't have lost
- [ ] adjusted / wouldn't lose
- [ ] have adjusted / won't lose
- [ ] had adjusted / didn't lose

**Hint:** Consider the compound tense used to discuss unreal past outcomes and missed business opportunities.

## Question 8
Before we finalize the distribution agreement, we need to _____ the payment terms with the finance team.

- [x] discuss
- [ ] discuss about
- [ ] discuss on
- [ ] discuss with about

**Hint:** Note that some transitive verbs take direct objects without requiring an intervening preposition.

## Question 9
All members of our international sales division look forward to _____ your representative at the upcoming trade expo.

- [x] meeting
- [ ] meet
- [ ] be meeting
- [ ] have met

**Hint:** Remember that the 'to' in this multi-word phrase functions as a preposition rather than part of an infinitive.

## Question 10
Could you please inform us when _____?

- [x] the shipment will clear customs
- [ ] will the shipment clear customs
- [ ] does the shipment clear customs
- [ ] is the shipment clearing customs

**Hint:** Embedded or diplomatic inquiries follow standard declarative word order rather than inverted question order.

## Question 11
During the negotiation session, the export director _____ the adjusted commission structure.

- [x] explained to us
- [ ] explained us
- [ ] explained about us
- [ ] explained for us

**Hint:** Pay attention to the required preposition when directing the verb 'explain' toward a person.

## Question 12
Under the standard freight policy, all damaged containers _____ thoroughly before they _____ to the overseas port.

- [x] are inspected / are dispatched
- [ ] inspect / dispatch
- [ ] are inspecting / are dispatching
- [ ] have inspected / will dispatch

**Hint:** Determine whether the subject performs the action or receives the action in formal logistics agreements.

## Question 13
The buyer considered _____ the trade agreement, but they refused _____ the additional tariff costs.

- [x] extending / to cover
- [ ] to extend / covering
- [ ] extending / covering
- [ ] to extend / to cover

**Hint:** Contrast the gerund pattern required after 'consider' with the infinitive pattern required after 'refuse'.
