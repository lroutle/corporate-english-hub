## 🧭 CEFR Quick-Diagnostic Matrix (Sales & Overseas Context)

| Area | A2 (Pre-Intermediate) | B1 (Intermediate) | B2 (Upper-Intermediate) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Spoken Fluency** | Can introduce products using rehearsed lines; pauses frequently during spontaneous negotiation. | Can explain deals, deliveries, and company info; occasional pauses for vocabulary when handling objections. | Fluent and persuasive; navigates client meetings smoothly; can reformulate during sensitive discussions. |
| **Tenses in Business** | Struggles to separate completed sales (*last quarter*) from ongoing accounts (*since 2022*). | Uses Past Simple well; occasionally misuses Present Perfect (*"I work with this client for 3 years"*). | Seamlessly shifts between past sales performance, current pipeline, and future projections. |
| **Diplomacy & Politeness** | Uses direct imperatives (*"Send me the PO"*, *"I want discount"*). | Uses basic modals (*"Can you send..."*, *"We need to change..."*). | Employs nuanced polite language & indirect questions (*"Could you clarify whether..."*, *"Would it be possible to..."*). |
| **Hypothetical Bargaining** | Avoids conditionals or says *"If you will give..."* | Manages 1st conditional (*"If you order 10 units, we will discount 5%"*); struggles with 2nd conditional. | Confident with 2nd conditional bargaining (*"If we extended credit terms, would you commit to...?"*). |

## 📝 Detailed Phase Instructions

### Phase 1: Conversation & Spoken Diagnostic (00:00 – 00:30)

#### Block A: Overseas Markets, Clients & Daily Sales Work (15 min)
1. *"Could you introduce your role in the overseas division? Which international markets or regions do you primarily manage?"*
   * *Target:* Geographic vocabulary, present simple for permanent responsibilities, third-person -s.
2. *"How long have you been handling these overseas accounts, and how has international demand developed recently?"*
   * *Target:* Present Perfect with *for/since* vs Past Simple for market changes.
3. *"How do you usually communicate with your foreign clients or distributors? Do you travel for meetings, or is it mostly online?"*
   * *Target:* Frequency adverbs, present continuous for current projects, modality (*have to, can*).
4. *"Tell me about a challenging overseas deal, contract negotiation, or delivery issue you resolved recently. What happened?"*
   * *Target:* Narrative past tenses (Past Simple, Past Continuous, Past Perfect).

#### Block B: Commercial Scenarios & Professional Opinions (10 min)
1. *"If you were responsible for opening up a completely new overseas market next year, which country would you target first and why?"*
   * *Target:* 2nd Conditional (*"If I were responsible... I would target... because..."*).
2. *"What are the biggest challenges when negotiating with international partners (e.g. cultural differences, pricing, lead times)?"*
   * *Target:* Expressing nuance, comparing and contrasting (*whereas, although, on the other hand*).
3. *"When you negotiate or write emails in English, where do you feel confident, and where do you feel you lack precision?"*
   * *Target:* Self-assessment, identifying learner's concrete grammar anxiety points.

### Phase 3: Immediate High-Impact Review (00:45 – 00:55)
Focus on **Present Perfect vs Past Simple in Commercial Communication**:
* **Rule in a nutshell:**
  * Finished time / specific date (*yesterday, in Q2, last week, three days ago*) $\rightarrow$ **Past Simple**.
    * *"We sent the revised quotation yesterday."*
  * Unfinished time / status up to now (*so far, already, yet, this month, since Tuesday*) $\rightarrow$ **Present Perfect**.
    * *"Have they confirmed the purchase order yet?"*
* **Quick Commercial Oral Drill:**
  1. *"We already (sign) the distributor agreement."* $\rightarrow$ *have already signed*
  2. *"Our overseas client (reject) the initial offer on Monday."* $\rightarrow$ *rejected*
  3. *"How many inquiries (you / receive) this week?"* $\rightarrow$ *have you received*
