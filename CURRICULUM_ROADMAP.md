# Tatra Trucks Interactive English Testing Suite: Master Curriculum Roadmap (120 Modules)

## 1. Executive Summary & Pedagogical Scope
The **Tatra Trucks Interactive English Testing Suite** is an instructional platform engineered specifically for automotive professionals at Tatra Trucks (Kopřivnice, Czech Republic). The suite bridges the gap between general professional English and high-precision automotive engineering, defense logistics, homologation, and international dealership sales.

### Key Demographics & Linguistic Profile
- **Target Audience:** Design engineers (chassis, powertrain, suspensions), assembly line team leaders, quality control inspectors, international tender managers, procurement specialists, and global service technicians.
- **Language Levels:** CEFR B1 (Threshold / Intermediate) and CEFR B2 (Vantage / Upper-Intermediate).
- **Core Contrastive Linguistic Problem:** Czech native speakers frequently suffer from mother-tongue transfer ("Czenglish"), including:
  1. *Aspect vs. Tense Confusion:* Using Present Simple or Continuous with *since/for* instead of Present Perfect (*"I work here since 2018"* vs. *"I have worked here since 2018"*).
  2. *Sequence of Past Actions:* Collapsing Past Perfect into simple past (*"The engineer noticed the valve wasn't calibrated"* vs. *"hadn't been calibrated"*).
  3. *Unnecessary Prepositions:* Overusing prepositions with transitive verbs (*"discuss about"*, *"explain me"*).
  4. *False Friends in Engineering & Commerce:* Confusing *control* (check / inspect) with *řídit / ovládat*, *actual* (current) with *aktuální*, *evidence* (records) with *důkaz*, *fabricate* (manufacture) with *vymýšlet si*, and *concurrent* (competing) with *souběžný*.
  5. *Passive Voice Omission:* Avoiding passive structures common in technical English manuals.

1. **Quality Assurance & Assembly:** Assembly line inspections, ultrasonic weld checks, torque tolerances, dynamometer procedures, 8D defect reports.
2. **Powertrain & R&D Engineering:** Central backbone tube chassis, swing half-axles, air suspension, Paccar vs. air-cooled Tatra V8 engines, CTIS.
3. **International Sales & Tenders:** Global dealer networks, government defense bids, Incoterms 2020, commercial presentations, trade fairs (IDET, Eurosatory).
4. **Procurement & Supply Chain:** Castings procurement, alloy steel specifications, vendor audits, delivery milestones, price negotiations.
5. **Homologation, Testing & Field Operations:** Extreme cold/heat testing, Dakar Rally support, UNECE safety standards, military STANAG certifications.
6. **After-Sales, Service & Training:** Technical service bulletins, field mechanic workshops, warranty disputes, CAN-bus fault code troubleshooting.
7. **General Automotive Manufacturing:** Plant logistics, safety standards, ISO 9001/14001, continuous improvement (Kaizen/5S).
