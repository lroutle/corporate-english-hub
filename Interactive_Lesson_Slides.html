Diagnostic Items 1 – 3

Core Tense Differentiation

1. Routine vs Current Project Right now our sales department _______ a key contract with an overseas distributor, but normally we _______ regional retail accounts.
A) negotiates / are handling B) is negotiating / handle C) negotiates / handle
2. Narrative Interruption While the export team _______ the logistics documents, the foreign buyer suddenly _______ to amend the payment terms.
A) prepared / asked B) were preparing / was asking C) were preparing / asked
3. Finished vs Unfinished Time I _______ with overseas distributors for five years now, but before joining this department I _______ domestic clients.
A) worked / have managed B) have worked / managed C) am working / managed
Diagnostic Items 4 – 6

Experience, Duration & Spontaneous Decisions

4. Commercial Experience vs Specific Past Deal "_______ to a major international trade fair in Germany or the Middle East?"
"Yes, I _______ our company stand at an expo in Frankfurt two months ago."
A) Have you ever been / attended B) Did you ever go / have attended C) Have you ever gone / attended
5. Duration of Ongoing Negotiations Our export manager looks exhausted because she _______ with our partners in Singapore nonstop since 6:00 AM.
A) is negotiating B) negotiates C) has been negotiating
6. Instant Commercial Decisions "Our client just sent an email refusing our shipping terms."
"Don't worry, I _______ their representative right away to clarify the situation."
A) will call B) am calling C) am going to call
Diagnostic Items 7 – 9

Regulations & Commercial Conditionals

7. Modals: Trade Rules & Regulations Under international export law, our department _______ provide an official certificate of origin before the shipment can clear customs.
A) should B) must / has to C) can
8. First Conditional (Sales Offer) If the overseas distributor _______ the full container load today, we _______ a 7% volume discount.
A) will order / provide B) orders / will provide C) orders / provide
9. Second Conditional (Hypothetical Bargaining) If our company _______ more flexible credit terms to foreign buyers, we _______ much faster market share abroad.
A) offers / will gain B) offered / would gain C) offered / will gain
Diagnostic Items 10 – 12

Past Review, Passives & Relative Clauses

10. Third Conditional (Past Missed Tender) If we _______ our sales pitch to their local business culture, we _______ the distribution tender last year.
A) adapted / wouldn't lose B) had adapted / wouldn't have lost C) had adapted / wouldn't lose
11. Contractual Passive Voice All export shipments _______ strictly according to Incoterms before they _______ to the port of entry.
A) insure / dispatch B) are insured / are dispatched C) are insuring / are dispatched
12. Relative Clauses This is the foreign distributor _______ warehouse network covers the entire Latin American market.
A) who B) which C) whose
Diagnostic Items 13 – 15

Verb Complements, Diplomacy & Prepositions

13. Verb Patterns in Negotiations The client considered _______ the contract for another two years, but they refused _______ the revised price index.
A) to renew / accepting B) renewing / to accept C) to renew / to accept
14. Diplomatic Indirect Questions "Could you clarify _______?"
A) when will the overseas shipment arrive B) when the overseas shipment will arrive C) when does the overseas shipment arrive
15. Dependent Prepositions in Sales Our team is responsible _______ developing overseas partnerships, and our quarterly targets depend heavily _______ market stability.
A) of / on B) for / in C) for / on

Immediate High-Impact Review

Present Perfect vs Past Simple in Commercial Writing

The Golden Rule for Client Updates and Export Reports

Past Simple: Completed Period

Used when the time is finished or a specific past date is mentioned.

yesterday, in 2023, last week, three days ago

✓ "We sent the revised quotation yesterday."

✓ "They visited our booth at the 2025 expo."

Present Perfect: Status Up to Now

Used when time is open, or the current status / result matters right now.

so far, already, yet, since, this week, ever

✓ "Have they confirmed the purchase order yet?"

✓ "We have already dispatched 300 units this month."
